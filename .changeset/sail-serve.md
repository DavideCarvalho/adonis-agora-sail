New `sail:serve`: runs the app in the background (`node ace serve --hmr`, detached in its own process group) once per worktree, so several people or AI agents working on the same worktree share one process. The first call starts it on the worktree `serve` port — the same dot-env `PORT` + worktree offset `sail:share` exposes, forced through `PORT` so it works with or without the core worktree-port patch — and waits until it answers; later calls reuse it (`reused: true`, with `pid`, `local` and `startedBy`), and a call that lands mid-boot waits instead of racing a second process. `--status` reports, `--stop` stops the whole group (SIGTERM, then SIGKILL after 10s), `--restart` is stop + start. `--watch` / `--no-hmr` pick the serve mode, `--by` (or `$SAIL_SERVE_BY`) records who started it, `--wait` bounds the boot wait. A port held by something sail did not start is refused; an app that dies or never answers fails with the last log lines in `hint`. State and logs live in `~/.sail/serve/<project>.{json,log}`. The managed `AGENTS.md` block now tells agents to use it instead of starting their own `serve`.
